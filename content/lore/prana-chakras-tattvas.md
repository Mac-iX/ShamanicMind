# Prana, Chakras & Tattvas
## A comparative guide to vitality, subtle anatomy, and elemental geometry

Prana, chakras, and tattvas arise within overlapping but historically distinct schools of Indian philosophy and practice. Western occultists adapted aspects of these systems in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. This essay uses **Israel Regardie's five tattva correspondences** as its visual reference while keeping other lineages clearly labeled.

### Prana and the three principal nadis
*Prana* signifies vital breath or life force in many yogic teachings. *Nadis* are subtle channels rather than anatomically verified vessels. **Ida** is traditionally lunar, receptive and cooling; **Pingala** is solar, activating and warming; **Sushumna** is the central channel through which Kundalini is said to ascend. These descriptions are doctrinal and symbolic, not findings of modern medicine.

### Chakras, wheels and lotus flowers
*Chakra* means wheel. Many Tantric descriptions use lotuses with characteristic petals: Muladhara 4, Svadhisthana 6, Manipura 10, Anahata 12, Vishuddha 16, Ajna 2, and Sahasrara a thousand. Familiar bija associations are LAM, VAM, RAM, YAM, HAM, with variations at Ajna and Sahasrara. The rainbow sequence red/orange/yellow/green/blue/indigo/violet is chiefly a modern standardization and is not universal to early chakra literature.

### Five tattvas in Regardie's Golden Dawn lineage
| Tattva | Element | Emblem | Qualities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prithivi | Earth | Yellow square | Cohesion, materialization, solidity |
| Apas | Water | Silver crescent | Liquidity, flow |
| Tejas | Fire | Red triangle | Heat, transmutation |
| Vayu | Air | Blue circle | Motion, vapor or gas |
| Akasha | Ether / Spirit | Black oval | Unity, limitlessness |

The five-element scheme is a subset of wider Indian philosophical use of the word *tattva* (principle or reality). Samkhya and various Shaiva metaphysical systems enumerate many more than five.

### The critical iconographic distinction
The Golden Dawn's **blue circle for Vayu and black oval for Akasha** differ from chakra-element yantras described in some Tantric sources. In a common Indian chakra mapping, Air at Anahata uses interlocking triangles, and Space at Vishuddha uses a circle. Do not silently interchange the two systems.

### Contemplation as research
The emblems provide visual prompts for attention, interpretation, and cultural comparison. Slow, ordinary breathing and brief study of one shape may support personal reflection. Neither the diagrams nor subjective sensations establish the anatomical existence of nadis or chakras, or medical benefit from occult visualization.
