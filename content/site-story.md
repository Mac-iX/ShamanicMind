# The lore I had to walk through

Some of my years were spent in the trenches—the inward work of meeting darkness without turning away. I learned to alchemize what I could not simply outrun, one question, one choice, one season at a time.

Nearly ten years of self-introspection led me through hundreds of books on psychology, philosophy, and mythology. Each offered another lens on a question I kept returning to: what is true within a reality shaped by perception?

Psychology gave me ways to examine the mind. Philosophy sharpened the questions. Mythology gave lasting images to the passage through shadow and change. Together, they drew me deeper into the layers of the matrix: the stories, beliefs, and symbols that shape what we notice and how we recognize our own nature.

ShamanicMind grew from that practice. A symbol is not a verdict; it is an invitation to look again. In a reading, we follow the patterns that speak to you, question what does not, and leave room for your own discernment.
